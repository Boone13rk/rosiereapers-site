Who Is Salami Sal?

Salami Sal is a fictional satirical character from the Reaper Six universe: an affable, menacing noir operator who sees every political maneuver as another slice in a much larger strategy.

His visual identity is deliberately anonymous — a completely shadowed face, blacked-out hands, a dark pinstripe suit, solid red fedora band, matching red tie and red rose boutonnière. He is a fictional character, not a depiction of a real person.

Sal's name comes from the political idea of "salami tactics" — gaining power incrementally, one slice at a time. The phrase is historically associated with the Communist takeover of Hungary in the years following World War II, when political opponents were gradually isolated and eliminated from power.

Reaper Six uses that history as the foundation for the character. Sal explains political strategy from inside his own noir worldview, while Reaper Six provides the larger commentary and historical frame.
